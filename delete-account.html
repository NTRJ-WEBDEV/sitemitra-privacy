SiteMitra Account & Data Deletion

SiteMitra users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your SiteMitra account to:

YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL

Use the subject:

SiteMitra Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your SiteMitra account. We may contact you to verify that you are the account owner before processing the request.

Data that may be deleted

Following verification, deletion may include personal account data such as:

  • SiteMitra account/profile information
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Personal profile information
  • Account authentication information, subject to the authentication provider's deletion process

Project records that may be retained

SiteMitra is a construction and project-operations system. Some records created while participating in a project may need to be retained to preserve legitimate project, custody, safety, security, contractual, legal, or audit history.

These may include:

  • Material custody and transfer history
  • Asset and cable operational history
  • Completed work and review records
  • Project audit records
  • Records required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or contractual obligations

Where appropriate, retained records may be anonymized or disassociated from the deleted user account while preserving necessary project history.

Processing period

Verified deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, security, contractual, and project-record retention requirements.
