SiteMitra

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SiteMitra collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SiteMitra mobile application.

Effective: 16 August 2026

1. About SiteMitra

SiteMitra is a construction and MEP site operations application designed to help project teams manage project information, people, work activities, assets, cables, material custody, identification tags, notifications, and related site records.

SiteMitra is currently being released for testing and field validation.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the information entered by your organization or project team, SiteMitra may collect and process the following categories of information.

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Account and authentication identifiers

Employment and Project Information

  • Employee or staff identification number
  • Position or job title
  • Discipline or department
  • Project and workspace membership
  • Project roles and permissions

Construction and Site Operations Data

  • Project names and project locations
  • Building, tower, wing, floor, area, and other location information
  • Work assignments and work status
  • Material custody and debit records
  • Asset information
  • Cable and electrical equipment information
  • Inspection or operational records where applicable
  • Remarks and notes entered by users

Tag and Identification Information

SiteMitra may create and process identifiers used for QR codes, barcodes, asset tags, cable tags, and material custody tags. These identifiers are used to identify and retrieve authorized project records.

Files and Imported Data

SiteMitra may allow authorized users to select and import files, such as Excel workbooks, containing project, people, location, asset, or work information.

Imported files may be processed to create project records. SiteMitra does not intentionally execute spreadsheet macros or use imported files for advertising purposes.

3. Camera Access

SiteMitra may request access to your device camera for features such as scanning SiteMitra QR codes or other supported identification tags.

Camera access is used only when required for these identification and site-operation features.

4. Notifications

SiteMitra may request permission to send notifications for reminders, project activities, work items, or other operational information.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

5. How We Use Information

We use information processed through SiteMitra to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide access to authorized projects and workspaces
  • Manage project team information
  • Manage project locations and construction records
  • Track work assignments and project activities
  • Manage assets, cables, materials, and custody records
  • Support QR and barcode identification workflows
  • Generate project-related labels and documents
  • Send reminders and operational notifications
  • Maintain operational history and accountability
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of SiteMitra
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service

6. Project and Organization Data

SiteMitra is designed for project-based collaboration. Information entered by one authorized project member may be visible to other authorized members of the same workspace or project according to their permissions.

Users should only enter project, employee, material, asset, and other information that they are authorized to manage.

7. Data Storage and Service Providers

SiteMitra uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to provide application functionality.

In particular, SiteMitra currently uses Supabase for services including authentication, database storage, and backend infrastructure.

Information required for shared project functionality may be transmitted securely from your device to these backend services.

Some information may also be temporarily stored locally on your device to support application functionality.

8. Data Sharing

SiteMitra does not sell personal information.

We may disclose or process information through service providers when necessary to operate SiteMitra, such as hosting, authentication, database, and infrastructure providers.

Project information may also be shared with authorized members of the same organization, workspace, or project as part of the normal operation of the application.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

9. Advertising

SiteMitra currently does not display third-party advertising and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled through SiteMitra.

Data transmitted between the application and supported backend services is transmitted using secure network connections where supported by those services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security, and users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

11. Data Retention

We may retain account and project information for as long as it is required to provide SiteMitra, maintain project history, support operational records, comply with applicable requirements, resolve disputes, and protect the integrity of project records.

Certain construction or project records may need to remain in project history even when an individual is no longer an active project member, because removing those records could affect the accuracy of project audit and custody history.

14. Children's Privacy

SiteMitra is designed for professional construction and project operations and is not intended for use by children.

We do not knowingly design SiteMitra to collect personal information from children for consumer use.

15. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information using infrastructure located in different countries or regions. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the security and contractual protections provided by those service providers and applicable law.
